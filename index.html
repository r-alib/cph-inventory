📋 Import stock from a table

as of

Paste cells from a spreadsheet: first column = product name (or barcode), then one column per location. With a header row the columns are matched by location name in any order; without one the order is Stockroom · TikTok 1 · Ebay · WhatNot · Slab & Single · Card Show. Nothing changes until you press Apply: each pasted number becomes the stock of that location at the end of the chosen day (today = now), and days after it are recomputed. Empty cells are skipped.
