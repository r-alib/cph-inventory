📋 Import stock from a table

as of

One field per column: paste the product names (or barcodes) into the first field and each location's column into its own field — row N of every field is the same product. Pasting a whole multi-column block into any field spreads it across the fields to the right. Nothing changes until you press Apply: each number becomes the stock of that location at the end of the chosen day (today = now), and days after it are recomputed. Empty cells are skipped.
